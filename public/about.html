About Lunia Foods

Lunia Foods started with a simple belief: the best food comes from people you can shake hands with. We partner directly with local farms, bakeries and makers to bring seasonal, honest food to your kitchen.

Our sourcing promise

Every product we stock is traceable to its grower. We prioritise organic and regenerative farms, small-batch producers, and short supply chains that keep money in our local community.

Fresh, not flashy

We deliver twice a week in reusable, plastic-free packaging, and we'll never sell you something we wouldn't serve at our own table.
